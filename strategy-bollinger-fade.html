The rule

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 35.5% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

Costs matter here: before costs it lost 29.7%. 2,870 trades each paid the spread and funding.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 51.5%.

It won 65% of its trades and still lost money: the losses were bigger than the wins.
