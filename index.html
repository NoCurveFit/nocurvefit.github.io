Our rules

Crypto is full of scams. So here is what we will never do.

Never your keys

We never ask for API keys, passwords, seed phrases or exchange logins. Anyone who does is not us.

Never your money

We don't trade for you, manage funds or hold deposits. We test rules. That's it.

Never a promise

A backtest is not a promise of future profit, and we never sell it as one.

Never tuned

We don't adjust your settings until the chart looks good. You get the honest number.

Questions

Which markets and charts can you test?

By default the 10 big coins: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX and SUI. On request also other coins and the gold, silver and stock markets listed on Lighter. Charts from 1 hour and slower (1h, 4h, daily).

What does "real costs" mean?

Every trade pays the real price spread of that market, and positions pay or receive funding like on a perpetual exchange. We also show the same rule on a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side, because fees decide everything for fast strategies.

Why is it called NoCurveFit?

Curve fitting means tuning a rule until it matches the past perfectly. It looks amazing on a chart and usually fails on the next one. We do the opposite: your settings, many coins, and a check of whether the result holds over time.

Can my strategy be private?

Yes. We never publish a customer's strategy or result without asking first.

Is this financial advice?

No. We report what a rule would have done in the past, after costs. What you do with it is your decision.
