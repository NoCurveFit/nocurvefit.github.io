The rule

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it made 19.7% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

Costs matter here: before costs it made 51.0%. 8,607 trades each paid the spread and funding.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 49.2%.

It was not stable over time: +40.1% in the first 6 months, −14.6% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It made money on 5 of the 10 coins.
