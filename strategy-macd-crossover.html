Does the MACD crossover strategy work on crypto?

No. Over 11 months it lost 21.2% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%. Tested with its textbook settings on 10 big coins, hourly data from 15 Nov 2025 - 15 Oct 2026, with real spreads and funding.

Verdict
The rule

Long when MACD(12,26,9) crosses up, short when it crosses down.

4h chart. Signals on the candle close, trades at the next price. $100 split over 10 coins, no leverage.

■ Just holding■ No costs■ After real costs

$100 split over BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX, SUI. Full one-page report →

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 21.2% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

Costs matter here: before costs it lost 18.0%. 1,650 trades each paid the spread and funding.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 32.9%.

It was not stable over time: −28.7% in the first 6 months, +10.4% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It made money on 2 of the 10 coins.
