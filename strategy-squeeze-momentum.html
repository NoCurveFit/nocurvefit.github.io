The rule

Trade the momentum when the squeeze fires, exit when momentum weakens.

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it made 4.7% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 1.6%.

It was not stable over time: −6.9% in the first 6 months, +12.4% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It made money on 6 of the 10 coins.
