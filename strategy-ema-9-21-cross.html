No. Over 11 months it lost 24.2% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%. Tested with its textbook settings on 10 big coins, hourly data from 15 Nov 2025 - 15 Oct 2026, with real spreads and funding.

Verdict
The rule

Long when EMA 9 is above EMA 21, short when below.

1h chart. Signals on the candle close, trades at the next price. $100 split over 10 coins, no leverage.

■ Just holding■ No costs■ After real costs

$100 split over BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX, SUI. Full one-page report →

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 24.2% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

Costs matter here: before costs it lost 17.2%. 3,712 trades each paid the spread and funding.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 47.5%.

It was not stable over time: −32.9% in the first 6 months, +13.0% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It made money on 3 of the 10 coins.
