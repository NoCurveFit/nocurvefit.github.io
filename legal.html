Terms of use

NoCurveFit provides backtests: reports of how a trading rule would have performed on historical data, using the method described on the Method page. A test is delivered as a one-page report.

You are responsible for the rules you send and for any trading decisions you make. We may decline a test, for example when it needs data we don't have; in that case you don't pay. Paid tests that we fail to deliver are refunded.

Reports are for your own use. Public results on this site may be shared with a link to NoCurveFit.

Risk disclaimer

Backtests describe the past. They are not a promise or prediction of future results. Trading, especially with leverage, can lose all of your money. Nothing on this site or in a report is financial, investment or tax advice, and nothing is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Our data and cost models are approximations of real trading. Read the limitations on the Method page before relying on any result.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no tracking scripts and no cookies of its own. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

When you send a strategy, we keep your message and your report to deliver the test and answer follow-up questions. We never sell or publish your strategy without asking. Reports are shared as unlisted links that are not shown on the site or in search engines. To have your data or report deleted, email nocurvefit@proton.me.

We will never ask for your API keys, passwords, seed phrases or exchange logins.
