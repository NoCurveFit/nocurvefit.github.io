Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 33.0% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 36.2%.

It was not stable over time: −39.8% in the first 6 months, +11.2% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.
