Verdict
The rule

Buy a close over the upper band(20,2), short under the lower, exit at the middle.

4h chart. Signals on the candle close, trades at the next price. $100 split over 10 coins, no leverage.

■ Just holding■ No costs■ After real costs

$100 split over BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX, SUI. Full one-page report →

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 17.5% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 23.3%.

It was not stable over time: −25.3% in the first 6 months, +10.5% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It made money on 2 of the 10 coins.
