Verdict
The rule

Stoch RSI cross under 20 with the 200 EMA trend, 1.5 ATR stop, 2 ATR target.

1h chart. Signals on the candle close, trades at the next price. $100 split over 10 coins, no leverage.

■ Just holding■ No costs■ After real costs

$100 split over BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX, SUI. Full one-page report →

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 12.6% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

Costs matter here: before costs it lost 6.5%. 2,552 trades each paid the spread and funding.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 32.3%.

It was not stable over time: −13.7% in the first 6 months, +1.3% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It made money on 2 of the 10 coins.
