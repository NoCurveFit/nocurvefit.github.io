Method

How we test. All of it.

If you can't see how a backtest was made, you can't trust it. This is exactly what happens to every strategy we test.

DataExecutionCostsPortfolioOver timeVerdict rulesWhat we can't testLimitations

Data

Hourly candles (open, high, low, close) for 129 markets listed on Lighter, a zero-fee perpetual futures exchange: crypto, forex, metals, energy, indices and stocks. The main test period is 15 Nov 2025 - 15 Oct 2026: about 8,000 hourly candles per market. Some newer markets have a shorter history; the market list shows each one.

4-hour and daily charts are built from the hourly candles. We can test 1-hour charts and slower, not 15-minute or 5-minute charts.

Execution

A signal is decided on the close of a candle and the trade happens at the next price. The rule never sees a candle before it closes, so it can't peek into the future.

Stops and targets are checked inside each candle using its high and low. When a candle touches both the stop and the target, we assume the stop was hit first (the worse case). If price gaps through a stop, the fill is at the open, not at the stop price.

Costs

Lighter charges no trading fees for standard accounts, but every trade still crosses the spread between buyer and seller. We charge each market's real spread on every entry and exit.

Strict spread (our default since 21 Oct 2026): spreads widen exactly when prices move fast, which is when many rules trade. So the spread on each candle is scaled by that candle's range compared to its usual range over the last 30 days, between 1x and 5x.

Funding: perpetual futures pay funding. We use Lighter's default rate: longs pay shorts about 0.00125% per hour (about 11% a year).

Normal exchange: every report also shows the same rule with a 0.05% fee on entry and exit, like a typical centralised exchange taker fee.

Portfolio

Unless you choose your own markets, we test 10 coins: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX, SUI. $100 is split equally at the start ($10 per coin) and never rebalanced. No leverage. Each coin trades the rule on its own.

Over time

A rule that only worked in one stretch of the chart is not reliable. Every report splits the period: the first 6 months (15 Nov 2025 to 15 May 2026) against the last 5 months (15 May to 15 Oct 2026). We also show how many months made money.

We never tune your settings to our data. You get the result of your rule as you wrote it. That's the whole point of the name.

Verdict rules

  • WORKS: profit after costs over the whole period, profit in both the first 6 and the last 5 months, better than buying and holding the same coins, and profit on at least 7 of the 10 coins.
  • SHAKY: profit after costs over the whole period, but at least one of the checks above failed.
  • DOESN'T WORK: a loss after costs over the whole period.

What we can't test

  • Rules that need volume: VWAP, OBV, volume profile, volume spikes.
  • Order book, liquidation or funding-rate signals.
  • On-chain data, news or sentiment.
  • Charts faster than 1 hour.
  • Anything before November 2025.

If your rule uses one of these, we tell you before testing and suggest the closest version we can test honestly.

Limitations

About 11 months of data covers a limited set of market conditions: a strong rule here can still fail later. Hourly candles hide what happens inside the hour. Spreads are modelled, not replayed from the real order book. Large orders would move the price more than our costs assume. A backtest is evidence, not a promise.
