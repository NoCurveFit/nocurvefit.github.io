FAQ · 23 questions

Questions, answered straight.

Can't find yours? Ask on X @NoCurveFit or email nocurvefit@proton.me.

General
What is NoCurveFit?

An independent service that tests trading strategies honestly. You send a rule, we run it on real market data with real costs and send you a one-page report with a clear verdict.

Who runs it?

An independent tester. It's anonymous on purpose: the numbers should speak, not a personality. Every method detail is public on the Method page.

Why the name?

Curve fitting means tuning a rule until it matches the past perfectly. It looks amazing on a chart and usually fails on the next one. We do the opposite.

Do you use AI?

Yes, AI tools help run the tests and write the code for each rule. The method, the data and the verdict rules are fixed and public.

The test
What do I need to send?

When you enter, when you exit, any stop-loss or target, which chart (1h, 4h or daily), long only or long and short, and which coins. Plain words are fine. The submit page has a builder.

Can I send a TradingView script?

Yes, paste the Pine Script as text. We translate it and tell you if any part had to be approximated. Please don't send files or links.

What if my rule is missing a detail?

We use the textbook default (for example RSI length 14) and tell you which one we used. If the core of the rule is unclear, we ask one short question first.

How long does it take?

Within 24 hours. Most tests are done much sooner.

Can I test a change afterwards?

Yes. One change is free: another chart, a stop-loss, other coins. More variations are $5 each.

Data & markets
Which markets can you test?

129 markets listed on Lighter: crypto, forex, metals, energy, indices and stocks. The default is 10 big coins. See the Markets page for the full list.

Which period?

15 Nov 2025 - 15 Oct 2026, hourly candles. Some newer markets start later.

Can you test 5-minute or 15-minute charts?

No. Our data is hourly, so we test 1-hour charts and slower. We'd rather say no than fake it.

Can you test volume-based rules like VWAP?

No, our data has no volume. We'll suggest the closest version we can test honestly.

Results & verdicts
What do WORKS, SHAKY and DOESN'T WORK mean?

WORKS: profit after costs in both halves of the period, better than buy and hold, and profit on at least 7 of 10 coins. SHAKY: profit, but fails one of those. DOESN'T WORK: a loss after costs.

My strategy works live. Why does your test say it doesn't?

Common reasons: a different period, fewer coins, no costs in your own test, or a lucky stretch. The report shows each piece so you can see where the difference comes from.

Does a WORKS verdict mean I'll make money?

No. It means the rule held up on the past data we have, after costs. Markets change. Start small if you trade it.

Do you share my results?

Never without asking. Public results on this site are popular strategies we chose to test ourselves.

Pricing & payment
How much does it cost?

The first 5 tests are free. After that $15 per test, paid in USDC.

When do I pay?

After we confirm your rule can be tested. You get the payment address with your order. If we can't test it, you don't pay.

Do you offer refunds?

If we can't deliver the test, you get your money back.

Safety & privacy
Will you ever ask for my API keys or seed phrase?

Never. We don't need access to anything. Anyone asking for keys, passwords or seed phrases in our name is a scammer.

Do you trade for me or manage money?

No. We test rules and, on request, build bots you run yourself. We never hold or move your funds.

Where do reports live?

You get your report as an unlisted link that isn't shown anywhere on the site or in search engines. Ask us and we delete it.
