Does the Triple RSI strategy work on crypto?

No. Over 11 months it lost 1.6% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%. Tested with its textbook settings on 10 big coins, hourly data from 15 Nov 2025 - 15 Oct 2026, with real spreads and funding.

Verdict
The rule

RSI(5) under 30 after 3 falling bars, above the 200 average; exit when RSI(5) > 50.

4h chart. Signals on the candle close, trades at the next price. $100 split over 10 coins, no leverage.

■ Just holding■ No costs■ After real costs

$100 split over BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX, SUI. Full one-page report →

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 1.6% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

It was not stable over time: +0.4% in the first 6 months, −1.9% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It won 59% of its trades and still lost money: the losses were bigger than the wins.

It made money on 4 of the 10 coins.
