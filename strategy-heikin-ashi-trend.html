Does the Heikin-Ashi trend strategy work on crypto?

No. Over 11 months it lost 10.3% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%. Tested with its textbook settings on 10 big coins, hourly data from 15 Nov 2025 - 15 Oct 2026, with real spreads and funding.

Verdict
The rule

Long when the Heikin-Ashi candle is green, short when red.

4h chart. Signals on the candle close, trades at the next price. $100 split over 10 coins, no leverage.

■ Just holding■ No costs■ After real costs

$100 split over BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE, LINK, AVAX, SUI. Full one-page report →

Why

What the numbers say.

Over 11 months it lost 10.3% after real costs, while just holding the same coins lost 13.9%.

Costs matter here: before costs it made 2.6%. 5,000 trades each paid the spread and funding.

On a normal exchange with a 0.05% fee per side it would have lost 45.6%.

It was not stable over time: −10.7% in the first 6 months, +0.5% in the last 5. Tested on only one of those stretches, it would look very different.

It made money on 2 of the 10 coins.
